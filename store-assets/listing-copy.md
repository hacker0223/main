## Description (4000 max)

```
Summit is a stock research app that shows its work.

Most investing apps hand you a number and hope you don't ask where it came from. Summit does the opposite: every statistic tells you how it was calculated, how accurate it has actually been, and what it cannot tell you. It doesn't execute trades — so it has no reason to push you into them.

RESEARCH THAT EXPLAINS ITSELF
• Live quotes, fundamentals, technicals, news, and SEC filings from EDGAR
• A transparent risk score — 45% historical volatility, 30% beta, 25% valuation, with the breakdown visible
• A 30-day probabilistic price range built from the stock's own volatility, clearly labelled as a statistical range and not a forecast
• Tap any metric for a plain-English definition, so you can learn RSI or price-to-book without leaving the screen

PATTERN LAB
Find real historical chart windows shaped like the one you're looking at, and see what actually happened next — a frequency count from genuine precedents, never a prediction. Every classifier estimate ships with its backtested accuracy attached, and tapping that number explains exactly how it was measured, including the purged train/test split used to prevent lookahead bias.

DEVIL'S ADVOCATE
State your read of a chart and Summit argues the strongest case against it, then names what would confirm or invalidate each side. Built to stress-test your thinking rather than agree with you.

TIME MACHINE
Invest fake money at any point in real market history. Buy in at 2000 prices, fast-forward a day, a month, or a decade at a time, and watch what your decisions actually produced. Play single-stock runs or build a portfolio.

Or enter a generated market: 20 invented companies with their own sectors, their own news cycle, and seven days to trade. Every run generates a completely new world, and the headlines genuinely move the prices. Nothing in it refers to a real security.

Completed runs land on the Hall of Fame, ranked by percentage return so a $500 run and a $50,000 run compete on equal footing.

CHART SANDBOX
Practice reading price action with no live money and no real symbols. Draw candles by hand, add indicators, replay a chart bar by bar, and get pattern commentary on what you built.

COMPARE
Line up to four tickers side by side, with a real statistical read on how any two of them move together — validated with a Monte Carlo significance test rather than an eyeballed correlation.

BUILT HONESTLY
• No ads, no ad-tracking SDKs, no data sold to anyone
• Your watchlist, alerts, and preferences stay on your device
• An account is optional and only needed to save Time Machine runs
• Summit never tells you what to buy or sell, and never executes a trade

Summit is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not financial advice, and Summit is not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. Simulator results use no real money and are not indicative of real investment performance. Market data is provided by third parties and may be delayed or inaccurate.
```
